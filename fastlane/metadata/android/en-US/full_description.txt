Lantern Sudoku is a calm, classic 9x9 sudoku for short, quiet sessions. It has no ads, no account and no game over, and it works fully offline.

<b>Hints that teach, not just tell</b>
When you're stuck, a hint lights the next step instead of giving the answer away. You choose how much help you want:
• Where to look: the area of the next step.
• Why: the technique by name, with a short explanation and the cells that matter highlighted.
• The digit, only if you ask for it.
If a digit on the board is wrong, the hint says so first, so you never chase a step built on a mistake. Every technique has its own Learn page, from singles, pairs and triples to pointing, box/line reduction and X-Wing. It's a gentle way to learn sudoku as a beginner, or to finally understand the moves you've been guessing.

<b>Honest difficulty</b>
• Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert.
• Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone, with no guessing.
• Each puzzle is graded by the hardest technique it needs, so a level means the same thing every time.
• More than 11,000 puzzles come with the app. Nothing is downloaded.

<b>A daily sudoku</b>
• One puzzle a day, the same for everyone, with a gentle streak and days played.
• Catch up on past days you missed.
• Share a spoiler-free result card if you like. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

<b>Easy on the eyes</b>
• Large, clear digits, plus a Larger digits option.
• Light, Dark and two high-contrast themes.
• Works with large system text and TalkBack.

<b>Never punished</b>
• Mistakes can be shown or hidden. A mistake limit is optional and off by default.
• Your game is saved after every move.
• Undo, redo, erase, and pencil-mark notes that tidy themselves when you place a digit.
• A calm summary when you finish, and statistics per level.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account.
• No internet access, so it plays the same on a flight or in a basement. The only permission is Google Play billing, used only if you choose to leave a tip.
• Optional tips in Settings › Support Lantern. They unlock nothing: every feature stays free for everyone.
• Your games stay on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android may copy them to your own Google account; the app itself never uploads anything.

<b>Free and open source</b>
Lantern Sudoku is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). There are no in-app purchases. The source code is public on GitHub and the app is also on F-Droid.

Made by one developer who wanted a sudoku that respects your time and attention. Feedback is welcome by email or on GitHub.
