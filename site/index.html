Lantern Sudoku

Calm, readable, offline sudoku

No ads, no account, no tracking and no game over. Honest difficulty, and hints that teach the next step instead of just filling in a number.

  • Four levels, from Easy to Expert (X-Wing), every puzzle solvable by logic.
  • A daily puzzle with a gentle streak.
  • Light, dark and high-contrast themes, larger digits and TalkBack labels.
  • No internet permission: it works anywhere.
