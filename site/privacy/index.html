Lantern Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · applies to Lantern Sudoku 1.0 for Android, from Google Play, F-Droid and GitHub

In short: Lantern Sudoku collects no data. It has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. Your games stay on your phone; Android's own backup may copy them to your Google account.

1. Information we collect

None. Lantern Sudoku does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. The app asks Android for no permissions at all, including internet access, so it cannot send anything over the network. There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

2. What the app stores on your phone

To let you continue a puzzle and see your progress, the app keeps, in its private storage on your phone:

  • your games in progress and finished games (the board, your notes, time, hints used and mistakes),
  • your statistics and daily streak, which are worked out from those games,
  • your settings (for example theme, larger digits and the mistake limit).

This data stays on your phone until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

3. Android backup and device transfer

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the data above to your Google account, and may move it to a new phone when you set one up. This is handled by Android and Google under Google's privacy policy; Lantern Sudoku itself never uploads anything. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

4. Sharing

When you choose to share a daily result, the app gives a short text (the date, level, time, hints and mistakes, and a grid of coloured squares) to the app you pick. That app receives the text and handles it under its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

5. Crash and performance data

The app contains no crash or performance reporting. If you have allowed it in your phone's settings, Google Play may give developers aggregated, anonymous app stability statistics (for example crash rates). These never include your games.

6. Children

Lantern Sudoku does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

7. Feedback

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to reply and to improve the app.

8. Changes

If anything changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the version that changes it.
